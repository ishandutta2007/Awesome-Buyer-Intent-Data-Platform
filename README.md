## 📌 Ecosystem Overview & Market Landscape

A curated, SEO-optimized list of **B2B Buyer Intent Data Platforms**, **Account-Based Marketing (ABM) tools**, **Revenue AI Intelligence**, and **Open-Source Lead Scoring Infrastructure**. 

Buyer intent platforms aggregate web-wide behavioral signals — content consumption, search keywords, hiring patterns, tech stack changes, and community activity — allowing revenue operations and growth marketing teams to identify in-market accounts actively evaluating solutions.

### 📊 Market Size & Industry Structure
> 💡 **Market Size & Dynamics:** The global B2B Buyer Intent Data market is valued at approximately **$2.4 Billion (2026)** and is projected to expand at a CAGR of **17.4%** to reach **$5.8 Billion by 2030**. The sector is **moderately fragmented**, dominated by key category leaders (6sense, Demandbase, ZoomInfo, TechTarget) operating proprietary data co-ops alongside an emerging long-tail of specialized visitor-identification, web-scraping, and AI-driven signal detection startups.

## 🛠️ Frameworks for Custom Intent Engines

Building a custom, privacy-compliant buyer intent stack? Combine these modular open-source components:

1. 📥 **First-Party Data Collection**: Deploy `rudder-server` or `laravel-visitor` to capture high-intent website actions (pricing page views, doc reads, demo clicks).
2. 🤖 **Intent & Lead Scoring**: Connect `ai-lead-scoring-engine` for tri-dimensional scoring (Fit + Engagement + Intent) or `SaaSquatch Intelligence Hub` for fast AI-based tiering.
3. 🔎 **External Signal Monitoring**: Integrate `Bright Data Lead Enrichment` for hiring/tech stack signals and `reddit-intel-agent-mcp` for social pain-point discovery.
4. ⚙️ **CRM & Pipeline Sync**: Utilize `gtm-enrichment-pipeline` or `Inspector` to push score updates directly to HubSpot/Salesforce for automated SDR alerts.
